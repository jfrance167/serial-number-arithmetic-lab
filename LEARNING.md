# Learning record

Serial numbers have a finite encoded range but no distinguished arithmetic minimum/
maximum. A numeric decrease may be a defined serial increase across wrap. Half-space
pairs are unequal without a defined order. Even other pairs can form cycles, so a
language ordering interface must not imply a valid sorting relation.

A chain's individual additions can be defined although one addition of their total
is undefined; concrete final comparison may be greater/less/equal/undefined. Keep
these questions and statuses separate. Comparison ambiguity alone does not erase a
known encoded value; an undefined addition does.

Independent expectations matter: twenty hand fixtures were frozen before code, and
small-domain oracles use integer inequalities/enumerated cycles, not the production
formula. Each meaningful mutant had its own required failing assertion. Testing only
ordinary wrap cases would miss half-range and aggregate-collapse defects.

ECC review reproduced a real output gap: OS closed pipe raises OSError, locally
closed Python stream raises ValueError. Initial locally-closed help child exited1;
red evidence saved. Both classes are now handled, including diagnostics, with real
normal/-O children and fixed no-echo errors. Detaching a failed stream prevents a
shutdown flush from changing controlled exit2 to120. Short/flush seams are explicitly
simulated; not measurements of arbitrary OS short-write behavior.
